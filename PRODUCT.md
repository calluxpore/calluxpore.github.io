# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers, recruiters, academic peers and potential collaborators sizing up Samarth Reddy as a designer-researcher. They usually arrive on one essay (shared link, search, portfolio click-through), read some of it, then scan the index to judge range, depth and consistency of thinking.

## Product Purpose

A personal research blog ("Mind Palace") of long-form essays on designing for and with Artificial Intelligence, with a recurring focus on AI and human memory, perception and speculative futures. Success: a visitor finishes an essay, opens a second one, and leaves with a clear sense of the author's line of inquiry.

## Positioning

The writing is the evidence. Essays connect personal experience (moving to Canada, encountering a capybara) with research on memory and machine perception — written by a designer with two design master's degrees (OCAD Digital Futures, Coventry Product Design) who is building memory-support tools.

## Operating Context

- Posts are authored in Obsidian and pushed by `publisher.py`; `_posts/` is never hand-edited.
- Every post may have a cover image (with recorded dimensions), a WebP thumbnail, and an edge-tts MP3.
- Jekyll on GitHub Pages; links to an external main portfolio on Webflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must preserve:
- Listen-to-article TTS audio player on posts and audio badges in the list.
- Publishing activity calendar (heatmap with year selector) on the homepage.
- Category filter on the homepage and the d3 category graph page.
- Related reading, previous/next navigation, reading progress, RSS, SEO tags, light/dark theme.

Not binding: the current fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible, MonoLisa) may be replaced.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Samarth Reddy, Creative Technologist. Site name "Mind Palace" in the intro. The ✱ mark is current but not confirmed as binding.

## Evidence on Hand

36 published essays in `_posts/` with cover images in `_assets/images/`, the about page, and the hero image `assets/images/hero-doorway.jpg`. No testimonials, metrics or press — do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. The essay is the work: reading comfort outranks decoration.
2. Show a coherent line of inquiry across posts, not a feed.
3. Every essay should invite the next one.
4. Accessible by default, in both themes.
