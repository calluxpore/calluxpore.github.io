# Surface brief: blog (home, post, page, graph)

Scope: the whole Jekyll blog shell. Mode: Read. Audience: hiring and academic peers judging a designer-researcher through essays on AI and memory. Constraints: keep TTS audio player, activity calendar, category filter, graph, related/prev-next, RSS, light/dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The blog is a type specimen of one research practice: a visible construction grid holds every page and each essay is a numbered entry on it. Refuses the card-feed blog with thumbnails and pill tags.

OWN-WORLD: White sheet with 40px Crouwel-blue hairline grid (dark: ink sheet, blue grid at low alpha). Ink #111, Crouwel blue #0057ff structure and rules, signal orange #ff4f1f as the one accent plane. Chakra Petch caps for labels and the masthead, Chakra Petch mixed case for essay titles (readability adaptation for long titles), Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Square corners, 1px blue rules, one diagonal slash as the only rebellion.

STORY: Reader sees a numbered body of work, opens the newest entry, reads in a calm column, then follows the next number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (name/slash, nav, grid toggle). Left: the newest essay's number built cell-by-cell from the page grid 7 cells tall (5x7 per digit); right: its title, standfirst, meta, Read and Listen controls. Below the hero rule, the numbered index begins at once; the publishing calendar follows the index. Site name sits in the top bar as SAMARTH REDDY / MIND PALACE.

FORM: Grid Specimen, challenger chosen over list position 4. seed:bd29c43fd89cb4cf. Signature interaction: grid-visibility toggle as real state; numerals assemble cell by cell on load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
